A new path along the river

The riverside between Mill Bridge and the marina is getting a new walking and cycling path. Before we finalise the design, we want to hear how you use the waterfront today and what would make it better.

This survey takes about five minutes. Your answers go straight to the design team and shape the plan that goes to the council this autumn.
