Why are all three open at the same time?

Different people have different amounts of time and different things to say. Some want to answer a quick survey, some have a specific idea, and some just want to weigh in on where the money goes. Running all three together means you can take part in whatever way suits you — without waiting for a separate phase.

Do I have to do all three?

Not at all. Any one of them is a real contribution. The card at the top tracks what you've done so you can pick up where you left off.

What happens with my input?

The project team reads every idea and survey answer, and the budget vote directly shapes the shortlist the city council reviews. We'll publish a summary back here once participation closes.
