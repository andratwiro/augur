Department spaces

Each department gets its own scoped workspace — its team, its projects, its branding — while you keep a single roll-up of engagement across the whole organisation.

Spaces replace flat folders for large orgs. A folder only groups projects; a space also scopes managers, audiences and branding to a department, and can sync its team from your directory (e.g. Microsoft Entra / SharePoint). Folders still work inside a space.
