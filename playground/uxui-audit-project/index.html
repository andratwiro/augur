Who we audit for · and how we sample

Five people, two audiences, two recruiting funnels.

Staff · back office

  • Joan — high savviness, optimises for speed
  • Diane — low savviness, relies on the UI to guide her

Residents · front office

  • Rare participant — low confidence, mobile, 30-sec patience
  • Engaged resident — wants outcomes; returns only if the loop closed
  • Skeptic — needs trust & impact obvious up front

~3 users per distinct group (never one pooled "5"); residents recruited early — it's the long pole.

Representativeness

Depth 3 · benchmark, don't guess

Compare against the best — for what they're best at.

DELIBERATION

vs Polis

One-tap agree/disagree + a live consensus map. Lowest-friction contribution and richest deliberation at once — our biggest gap.

BACK OFFICE

vs EngagementHQ

Project templating/cloning and layered reporting. Worth matching for staff efficiency.

CLOSING THE LOOP

vs Decidim

Follow a proposal through visible stages — closure as a data model. The #1 trust failure sector-wide.
