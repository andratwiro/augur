Plant trees on Station Road

Northgate · one Saturday morning (3 hours) · 12 of 20 spots filled

Help line the new Station Road with lime and rowan trees as part of the mobility redesign. We’ll dig, plant and stake together, then mark each tree with the name of the street it belongs to.

Harbour clean-up crew

Harbour · monthly, second Saturday (half day) · 9 of 16 spots filled

Keep the quayside and the mouth of the Mere clear of litter so the water stays healthy. Joins up with the Harbour clean-up morning — a friendly crew, a coffee at the end, and a visibly cleaner waterfront.

Good for: anyone who loves the harbour and doesn’t mind a grabber and a hi-vis vest. All kit supplied.

Greening the schoolyards

Citywide · weekday afternoons in term time (2 hours) · 7 of 15 spots filled

Turn grey playgrounds into green ones. Following on from the Greening the schoolyards project, we’re planting hedges, fruit trees and pocket gardens with pupils at three primary schools.

Note: this cause involves working alongside children, so we’ll ask for a quick background check before your first session.

Market Square stewards

Old Town · market days, flexible shifts (3 hours) · 4 of 10 spots filled

Be a friendly face on Market Square. Stewards welcome visitors, water the new planters and tend the tree pits around the square — keeping the heart of the Old Town green and well looked after.

Riverside path wardens

Riverside · ongoing, your own pace (1–2 hours/week) · 6 of 12 spots filled

Adopt a stretch of the Mere’s riverside path and help keep it welcoming — sweeping leaves, weeding the verges and reporting anything that needs the council’s attention. Builds on the Riverside path study.

Good for: regular walkers and runners who’d like to give their favourite route a little care.
