A square for everyone

Submit your idea See the ideas
122 contributions

Market Square is the heart of the Old Town, and it's time we rethought it together. We're redesigning the square so it works for everyone — for the weekly market, for people stopping for a coffee, for children and for residents getting around on foot and by bike.

Right now the square is given over mostly to parking and through-traffic. Over the coming months we'll gather your ideas, test a few of them on site, and bring the community's priorities into a final design that goes to the council next year.

Everything you share here is public and helps shape the shortlist that goes to the next phase. Have a read of the questions below before you post, then add your idea or react to others.

How will my idea be used?

Every idea is read by the project team and grouped with similar suggestions. The most-supported, workable ideas move forward to the prioritisation phase, where residents help decide which ones to take into the final design.

What can I suggest?

Anything that fits within Market Square and its surrounding streets — seating, planting and trees, the weekly market layout, play, lighting, paving, cycle parking or how traffic moves through. Out-of-scope ideas are tagged and passed to the right team.

Who is running this project?

The City of Westmere — Engagement team, together with the Old Town public-space designers. Project updates are posted to followers as each phase opens.
