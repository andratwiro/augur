🧠 2025 Betting Table(s) 🧠

💭 It's that time of our planning cycle again! We're about to wrap four development tandems and are working on short-listing the priorities for the next cycle.

The objective is simple: we want to hear from you about what you perceive as the number-one problem blocking our impact or revenue opportunities. Alternatively, you might see this as an opportunity waiting to be seized. Whether it's an obstacle or a potential goldmine, your perspective is crucial.

34 participants
🗿 What should my submission include?

Name the single problem or opportunity you think we should focus on next cycle, and why it matters. One clear idea per submission works best.

Concrete examples — a customer quote, a lost deal, a recurring support theme — make your case far stronger than an abstract statement.

🐈 What context do I need to be aware of?

We're optimising for impact and revenue this cycle. Ideas that unlock a new use case, remove a blocker for a big market, or measurably improve retention are especially welcome.

🦔 What happens after I submit my idea?

Submissions are consolidated, prioritised together with the community, and reviewed by the Product Circle. The shortlist is then announced as the development plan for the cycle.
