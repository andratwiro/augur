Station Road walk & talk: see the plans on site

Come and see the proposals for Station Road where they'll actually happen. We'll walk the street together — from Northgate station down to the junction — stopping at the points where the biggest changes are planned: the new cycle lane, the widened pavements, the crossing by the school and the extra street trees.

At each stop the project team will explain what's proposed and why, and there's plenty of time to ask questions, point out what works for you, and flag anything we've missed. What we hear on the walk feeds straight into the next phase of "The future of Station Road", so this is a real chance to shape the design before it's fixed.

Wear comfortable shoes and dress for the weather — we'll be outside for the full hour and a half. The route is step-free and wheelchair accessible; if you have any access requirements or questions beforehand, get in touch with the engagement team and we'll make sure you're looked after.

Meeting point: outside Northgate station, Station Road (look for the Westmere — Have your say banner). No need to book, but registering helps us plan numbers.

Event date: 25 June 2026 from 5:30 PM to 7:00 PM.
