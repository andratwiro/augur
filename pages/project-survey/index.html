The big downtown survey

Take the survey
248 participants

Downtown Westmere — the Centre and Harbour districts — is where the city comes together to shop, work, eat and meet. But the way we use these streets is changing, and we want to plan the next decade with you, not for you.

This short survey asks one big question: what does downtown need to become more liveable? Your answers will shape the priorities we take into the Centre & Harbour action plan, from greener streets and the weekly market to cycling, parking and the quayside.

It takes about three minutes, your answers are anonymous, and every response counts. Have a read of the questions below, then have your say.

How will my answers be used?

Responses are analysed together and shared back as a public summary. The strongest themes feed straight into the Centre & Harbour action plan that goes to the council later this year.

Who can take part?

Anyone who lives, works, studies in or visits downtown Westmere. You don't need to live in the Centre or Harbour districts to have a say in their future.

Who is running this project?

The City of Westmere — Engagement team, with the downtown planning group. Follow the project and we'll let you know when the results are published.
