If Westmere could get one thing right over the next few years, what should we focus on first?

Perspectives gathers what residents across the six districts are saying about life in Westmere — the harbour and the Mere, Station Road, Old Town renewal, the participatory budget and our climate teams. Your voice helps set the agenda.

Share your view and see what neighbours are saying. To contribute, please sign in so your input is saved and the process stays secure and credible. You're welcome to add more than one perspective.

Daan Verhoeven Let us get back to the water The Mere is the heart of Westmere, but most of the quayside is still fenced off for the working harbour. I'd love a continuous walkway from the Harbour district round to Riverside — somewhere families can stroll, fish and watch the boats. Keep the harbour working, but give residents a proper edge on the water again.
Tom Bekker Slow the traffic on Station Road Cars come through Northgate far too fast at school run. We need safer crossings on Station Road and a proper cycle lane — my kids won't ride to the station the way it is now.
Clara Janssen Keep the Old Town for living in The renewal is welcome, but the Old Town can't become only cafés and holiday lets. Let's restore the cobbles and Market Square and still leave room for families and small shops to stay.
Marek Nowak Spread the budget across the districts The €1.2M can't all land in the centre. Let's set aside a share for each of the six districts so Lakeshore and Westside get a fair look in too — and make the vote easy to join offline.
Aisha Karimi More trees and shade in Westside Our climate team wants every street with a bit of green and shade for hot summers. Start with the school routes and the bare stretches of Westside — small planting, big difference.
Lena Okonkwo A Market Square for everyone The square is lovely but there's nowhere to sit. More benches, a bit of shade and somewhere for kids to play would bring people back — not just on market days. Let's redesign it together.
