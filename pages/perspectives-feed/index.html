If Westmere could get one thing right over the next few years, what should we focus on first?

Perspectives gathers what residents across the six districts are saying about life in Westmere — the harbour and the Mere, Station Road, Old Town renewal, the participatory budget and our climate teams. Your voice helps set the agenda.
