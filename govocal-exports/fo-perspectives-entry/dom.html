The Big Conversation

We’re creating a new way for community members of Greenville to speak up about what we want to fix in our region—and turn resident priorities into legislative action. Click the link below to see what others are saying and add your voice. It takes just 5–7 minutes.

What needs fixing? Click here to have your say!


How do you start?

  1. Scroll posts to see what your neighbors across the state are saying.
  2. Add your post by registering and answering: what do you want to fix?
  3. Like or dislike issues based on what matters most to you.
  4. Come back to see how the conversation evolves—and how you can play a role.


What comes next?

You play a critical role to:

  • Map issues
  • Vote on the most important issues
  • Read the report
  • Join small-group discussions

This will inform cross-partisan, people-driven legislative action in Fall — so watch this space!

Join the conversation!


﻿Why should you register?

When you provide a response, we ask you to register with an email address so we can keep you updated about Greenville actions.

We respect your privacy and will never sell your data. Your responses are anonymized.

Together, we can collaborate to shape a better future for our state.
