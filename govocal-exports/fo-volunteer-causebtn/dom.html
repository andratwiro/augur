Are you itching to start working as a volunteer at the City of Ostend? Then be sure to keep an eye on this page. All actions that require volunteers are listed here.

You can simply register by clicking on the 'I sign up' button for the volunteer work of your preference. Afterwards you will receive an email with more information and further arrangements will be made.

Good luck and thank you in advance for your interest!

Volunteer residential care centers 'Out and about with residents'

1 participant

You take the residents of the residential care center outside for a walk or do some shopping together.

WHAT DO WE EXPECT?

Do you like being outside and can you drive a wheelchair? And do you have a socially sensitive heart for older people?

WHAT DO WE OFFER?

An enthusiastic group of volunteers and genuine appreciation from the people you go out with. And a correct volunteer framework with the necessary insurance.

Volunteers for the Bicycle Taxi

3 participants

Several meeting centers in Ostend offer a bicycle taxi. As a bicycle taxi driver you transport less mobile people to and from the meeting center. Do you feel like putting your best foot forward, literally and figuratively? Then click on "I sign up".

Task content:

- You transport people with an electric bicycle taxi to and from the meeting center in the area.

- You are social and like to have a chat.

- You can count on a volunteer allowance.

Volunteer - Reader for the Book Visit project

4 participants

BoekVisit is a reading-at-home project. You read for a family at home and enchant them with beautiful stories and literally bring books into the living room. A visit to the library is also planned.

WHAT DO WE EXPECT?

You are 18 years old and have good Dutch reading and storytelling skills. You love reading and want to have a positive impact on the lives of your reading family.

WHAT DO WE OFFER?

A fascinating experience and great gratitude from your reading family. You will also receive the necessary support during the reading period and a lot of interesting information about books and the importance of reading aloud. All this within a correct volunteer framework with the necessary insurance.

Volunteers for the homework classes

3 participants

The Education Department of the city of Ostend organizes homework classes in the Westerkwartier, Vuurtorenwijk and Stene districts. In the homework classes, vulnerable primary school children are given the opportunity to do their homework in the best conditions.

WHAT DO WE EXPECT?

Do you have a heart for children and a healthy dose of patience? Can you encourage children and do you like to pat them on the back? Do you speak and read Dutch fluently? Then you are the volunteer we are looking for!

WHAT DO WE OFFER?

You will end up in a great volunteer team. You will receive professional support from an employee and free training. And a lot of recognition and gratitude from the children! We provide a correct volunteer framework and the necessary insurance.

Volunteers for the Growth House

1 participant

The Doorgroeihuis Oostende is looking for committed volunteers with a commitment to homeless people to fill a morning shift (7:00 AM to 9:00 AM), an evening shift (winter: 5:00 PM - 9:00 PM / summer: 7:00 PM - 9:00 PM :00) or a night shift (9:00 PM - 7:00 AM). The night shift is reimbursed at 50 euros per night worked.

You guarantee a warm welcome, a friendly conversation and logistical support during opening hours. You communicate cordially with fellow volunteers, community workers, clients and suppliers. You are cheerful, somewhat stress-resistant and a team player. Being understanding and non-judgmental is what you can do.

Short contacts such as serving a hot meal or pouring a cup of coffee contribute to establishing a long-term care relationship. Your welcome is one of the first steps in connecting an unmoored target group with social services and contributes to improving the quality of life of people experiencing homelessness.
