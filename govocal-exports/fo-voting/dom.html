Option Analysis + Voting: Redesigning the Main Square

The city is considering a major landscape architectural development. Currently divided in a peculiar four-square arrangement, three new proposals re-image our Public Square into a united green space design. Let us know which one you prefer!

The designers offer three radically different designs to the square. Deemed, The Frame, The Forest, and The Thread, the concepts address traffic and circulation matters to different extremes, while all providing elements of urban park goodiness.

Source: http://www.designundersky.com/dus/2009/12/22/rethinking-clevelands-public-square.html
