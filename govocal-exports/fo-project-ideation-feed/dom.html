Let's Reimagine Dorothea Dix Park

As part of the 2024 Master Planning process, Raleigh is redesigning many of the city's most iconic and well-known public spaces, including Dorothea Dix Park. Tell us what you and your community would like to see in the Park. What is the current park missing, and how can we make it a more inclusive, vibrant, and fun space?

Submit an idea below, or comment and vote on other ideas for what you want to see in the new City Park. Your ideas and feedback will determine which facilities make it into the park; winning ideas will be announced in December 2023!

The intention is to have a fully-fledged Calisthenic park built so that people can practice this sport to the limit. By building this park it can become a place where young and old can keep themselves in shape. Especially the youth is very interested in this.
