Submit your topics to the EAB

The Employee Advisory Board (EAB) represents employees and formulates advice to management, mainly on governance. The EAB consists of 6 rotating members from each team. 

Go Vocal has grown considerably over the last years and with more management layers in place, there is a potential for more engagement and communication between management and employees. The goal is to improve communication within CL and push for topics that will eventually improve our organisational culture as a whole.
