Survey: Redesigning Coffman Park

In 2024, our Urban Planning & Mobility teams are embarking on an exciting renovation project for Coffman Park to create a more enjoyable and vibrant recreation area for all residents. This is your opportunity to help shape the future of this beloved community space.

We want to hear from you! What would make Coffman Park a better place to relax, play, and connect with others? What amenities or features are missing, and how can we make this park more inclusive, sustainable, and fun for everyone?

  • Explore the proposed plans in the survey below, and share your thoughts. Your feedback will play a crucial role in determining the final design and features of Coffman Park.

What’s the first word that comes to mind when you think of Coffman Park?

31/50 - Short answer - optional

Most respondents associate Coffman Park with nature, greenery, and a sense of peacefulness or relaxation. Words like "nature" "green" and "peaceful" were mentioned most frequently. Many also described the park as "relaxing" "inviting" or "welcoming" and "fun". Other less frequent words included "spacious," "quiet," "clean," "safe," "picturesque," and "picnic." A few outlier responses described the park as "loud" or "abandoned" suggesting some negative perceptions, but these were rare. Overall, the dominant themes are positive, emphasizing natural beauty, tranquility, and a welcoming atmosphere.

What improvements would make Coffman Park more useful or enjoyable for you and your household?

43/50 - Long answer - optional

The most frequently mentioned improvements were more benches and seating areas and better lighting for safety and usability in the evenings. Many respondents also requested more shaded areas, either through trees or picnic pavilions.

There was also significant interest in improved restroom facilities more sports and exercise facilities and dedicated dog areas. Several respondents wanted more green space, lawns, and nature-focused areas.

Other suggestions included improved playgrounds more bins for trash water fountains food and drink options like a café or food trucks and improved maintenance. A few unique requests included a birdwatching tower a child-free zone and deeper water.

One respondent expressed satisfaction with the park as it is. Overall, the main trends are a desire for more seating, shade, lighting, restrooms, and facilities for both recreation and relaxation.

Why?

34/50 - Short answer - optional

Many respondents feel the current features (especially the skate park and courts) are underused, outdated, unattractive, or take up too much concrete space. Several mention that the area is mostly used by youth for hanging out or causing trouble, and not serving the broader community.

A strong trend is support for redesigning or modernizing the park to better meet current community needs, make it more versatile, accessible, and visually appealing. Some suggest reusing or repurposing materials rather than full demolition to avoid waste.

However, a notable minority oppose demolition, arguing that the existing features have potential, add unique character, or are valued by certain groups (especially skaters). Several respondents call for compromise—retaining or upgrading some features rather than removing everything.

In summary, most respondents support significant changes to make the park more attractive and useful, but there is also a clear desire among some to preserve or upgrade valued existing features rather than demolish them entirely.

Do you have any additional thoughts to share with us on the redesigning of Coffman Park?

35/50 - Long answer - optional

Respondents most frequently mentioned the need for more seating, especially along walking paths and in shaded areas. Improved walking paths and better lighting were also common requests. Many respondents emphasized maintaining and enhancing the park’s natural feel, with calls for more greenery, flowers for pollinators, and eco-friendly initiatives.

Requests for more restrooms appeared multiple times. Shade was another recurring theme, both for comfort and gatherings. Some respondents wanted more family-friendly activities and events while others asked for amenities like an outdoor library and better connections to nearby trails.

There were also comments about keeping the park open and not overcrowded with structures considering all age groups and incorporating sustainable water management. Several respondents expressed excitement or support for the redesign. A few preferred the park’s current character or had no additional suggestions.
