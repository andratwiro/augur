 Hola hola! It’s been some busy weeks! BUT: we have bets! The development priorities for the next 8 weeks are the following 1. Tandem 1: Optional, lazy registration – Making it easier for people to participate without mandatory account creation

2. Tandem 2: Project/method dashboards – Unlocking method-level dashboarding and insights to better demonstrate consultation impact and drive stickiness of our product amongst pms and admins

3. Tandem 3:

  • Part 1: 2 week sprint on issue reporting/Mängelmelder to unlock an MVP for this new usecase for our tool and validate potential in DACH and other markets in Q4. 
  • Part 2: Deliberation for America 1 – Building out deliberation capabilities specifically to support the A1 process (direction will be concretized by next week via meeting in NYC)

4. Quality Quest – Continuing our focus on product quality and stability; with allocated time to small improvements around platform progress reportsPlus ongoing work on Accessibility improvements.
