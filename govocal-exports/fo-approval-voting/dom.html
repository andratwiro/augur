Approval Voting: Decide on the future of the Greenwood cemetery

We are excited to invite you to participate in shaping the future of our historic Greenwood Cemetery. As a cherished landmark that holds the memories of generations past, we believe it's essential to preserve its legacy while embracing modernity. After careful consideration, we have curated three distinct development ideas for the cemetery, each envisioning a unique path forward. Your voice matters, and we encourage you to explore the detailed plans, captivating renders, and thoughtful proposals on our website. Take this opportunity to play an integral role in shaping the destiny of Greenwood Cemetery by casting your vote for the plan that resonates most with your heart.
