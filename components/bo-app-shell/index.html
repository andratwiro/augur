Reuse: scope markup under .gv-bo — it remaps --gv-tenant-* to GoVocal's fixed back-office palette, so existing primitives (.gv-btn.admin-dark, .gv-iconbtn, inputs, focus) render in back-office colours automatically. The shell adds only what the front office lacks: .gv-bo-shell, .gv-bo-side, .gv-bo-topbar, .gv-bo-tabs. Phase ribbon, sub-tabs, tables and stat cards are separate components that mount in the content area.
