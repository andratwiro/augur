Community Monitor

The “City at a glance” satisfaction module — an ongoing survey that surfaces as a top modal/banner on the homepage and asks how residents feel about governance and public services as a 5-point emoji sentiment scale. Grounded on the live .modalcontent / #community-monitor-modal-title / #<q>-linear-scale-option-N.

City as a place to live

This ongoing survey tracks how you feel about governance and public services.

Takes 2 minutes

Reuse: the shell is the standard modal abstraction — .gv-modal-overlay → .gv-modal.size-monitor (460px card), __header/__title, top-right __close, scrollable __body. The body is the new .gv-monitor module: __intro (muted cool-grey copy) + __question + __count.

The new variant: .gv-sentiment-scale is a horizontal 5-emoji linear scale (each __opt a 56px column, tinted in var(--gv-tenant-primary), emoji over __cap caption). It is a sibling of the survey kit’s vertical .sv-sentiment — built fresh, not a mutation of it. Options are aria-pressed buttons; one selects at a time. Faces are native full-colour emoji glyphs (😣 🙁 😐 🙂 😀) via the .gv-sentiment-scale__face.is-emoji variant — matching the real yellow-face linear scale; selection chrome stays on the face.
