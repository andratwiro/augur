Project-template card & facet filter rail

The two pieces of the back-office "From a template" gallery (/admin/projects/new). .gv-bo-templatecard is the grey-blue bordered template tile — measured bg #F2F4F5, 1px #EAEAEA, 3px radius, 16px pad — distinct from the white floating .gv-bo-card. .gv-bo-facetgroup is the collapsible filter disclosure that fills the gallery's left rail.

Template cards · .gv-bo-templatecard

Image (118px) + 21/500 navy title + 14/400 grey desc + two stacked actions (.secondary-outlined "Use template" / .admin-dark "More details").

Facet filter rail · .gv-bo-facetgroup

Three disclosure groups; the first is expanded (chevron flipped). Click any head to toggle.

Reuse: a template tile is .gv-bo-templatecard holding a __img (background-image), a __title, a __desc, and a __actions column of two .gv-btn buttons. A filter group is a .gv-bo-facetgroup wrapping a <button class="gv-bo-facetgroup__head" aria-expanded> (label + __chev) over a __body; the chevron rotates on aria-expanded="true". Both render under the .gv-bo body scope so --gv-bo-primary resolves. Source-grounded on r1-px-projects-new-template.
