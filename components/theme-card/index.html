Theme card

Ranked Perspectives category card with an emoji swatch, name, response count and a mini share bar; the first one shows the active selection.

Ranked category list (first card .is-active)

Selection: add .is-active to mark the chosen category; only one card carries it at a time.

Swatch tint: set style="--gv-themecard-swatch: rgb(…)" on the card to colour the emoji chip per theme.

Share bar: the __bar inner <i> takes a width:% and its own --gv-themecard-bar fill colour to show each theme's share of responses.
