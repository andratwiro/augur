Cookie-consent modal

The global cookie blocker rendered as the standard GoVocal dialog — a content variant on the .gv-modal shell. Source-grounded on Stadt Wien (.modalcontent / #cookie-modal-title / data-cy=e2e-accept-cookies-btn): leading product cookie icon (tenant-tinted), title + body + policy link, and a 3-action footer (Manage / Reject / Accept).

Reuse: the shell is the standard .gv-modal-overlay → .gv-modal (650px card, 3px radius, rgba(0,0,0,.75) scrim, top-aligned) — unchanged. The cookie prompt only adds the content: .gv-cookie__content (30px pad) holding a tenant-tinted .gv-cookie__icon (the cookie glyph, real fill="#ff5a64" → --gv-tenant-primary), a __title (21/700/#333), a __body (16/400/24/#333) with a tenant-primary __link.

3-action footer: .gv-modal__footer (pad 16px 28px, reusable on any modal) — Manage (.gv-btn.text ghost, pushed left via .gv-cookie__manage), then Reject + Accept (.gv-btn.primary, tenant-filled). No top-right close — a consent prompt blocks the surface until the resident answers. Cross-tenant SYSTEM: every tenant routes its consent prompt through this dialog; the skin (icon tint, font, language) re-themes per ?theme=.
