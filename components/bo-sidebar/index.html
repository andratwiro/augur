The persistent staff navigation. Two states: extended (224px, icon + label) and a collapsed rail (80px, icon-only) that GoVocal switches to at ≤1200px. Resize this window past 1200px to see the left one adapt; the right one is forced to the rail with .is-rail.

Reuse: the markup is one <nav class="gv-bo-side"> (template below). Default is extended; add .is-rail for the rail. To make it adapt automatically, mark it data-gv-side-auto and include the matchMedia snippet (it toggles .is-rail on the sidebar and any .gv-bo-shell at 1200px). Icons are 24px link-blue; labels white; the active item is a dark cell (inset+rounded in the rail). Notification count overlaps the bell when collapsed.
