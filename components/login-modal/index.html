The abstraction: .gv-modal-overlay (scrim, toggle .is-open) wraps the .gv-modal card — its parts are __header + __title, the top-right __close icon button, and a scrollable __body. Width via --gv-modal-w (.size-s 420 / default 500 / .size-l 680).

Wiring: the card is the dialog — role="dialog", aria-modal="true", and aria-labelledby pointing at the __title id. Esc and a backdrop click close it here; real focus-trap and focus-restore are the dev team’s job on the live codebase.

Body is pure primitives: this login uses .gv-label + .gv-input, the .gv-or labeled divider, and .gv-btn (primary Continue, white full Google) — swap the body for any content to reuse the modal.
