Extra survey CTA

A survey linked into a project page or the Content Builder, separate from the phase's own method. It renders either as a button or as a card. The button uses the same primary / secondary-outlined pair as the participation box.

Buttons: .gv-btn.primary (filled tenant) or .gv-btn.secondary-outlined (neutral outline) — the same pair as the participation box, so the two CTAs sit together on a page without clashing.

Card: sized for legibility by default — big type, one clear action, the icon always paired with a word. The SURVEY tag is tenant-tinted with an AA-safe darkened ink.
