The Board of Aldermen is listening!

The Board of Aldermen's public engagement platform makes it easier for residents to share ideas with elected officials, connect with other residents, and stay up-to-date on policies and projects taking shape in St. Louis.

What is Speak Up, St. Louis?

Speak Up is where residents and the Board of Aldermen meet online. Browse active projects, react to and comment on ideas, take part in surveys, and follow the policies moving through City Hall — all in one place.

Who can take part?

Anyone can read along. To post an idea, react, or answer a survey you'll be asked to create a free account so the team knows your contribution comes from a real resident.

What happens to my contribution?

Every contribution is read by the project team and grouped with similar input. Aldermen use what's shared here to shape the policies and projects they bring forward, and project updates are posted back to participants as each phase opens.
