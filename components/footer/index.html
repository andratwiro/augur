Source-grounded on GoVocal's real <footer id="hook-footer">: a secondary-nav row of legal links — one is the Cookie-Einstellungen button — plus the "Ermöglicht durch go·vocal" powered-by attribution (real GoVocal wordmark). No tenant logo (that lives in the header).

Use it: copy the <footer class="gv-footer"> block. The legal links are a semantic <ul> inside a <nav aria-label="Secondary"> landmark; Cookie-Einstellungen is a real <button> (it opens the cookie dialog), the rest are links.

go·vocal logo: <img class="gv-powered-logo" src="../../skills/govocal-ui/govocal-logo.svg"> — GoVocal's own brand mark in its muted footer grey (it doesn't re-theme per city). Copy govocal-logo.svg into the prototype folder. Keep this attribution on resident-facing pages.

Links: middot separators are automatic (.gv-footer__links li:not(:first-child)::before). They wrap on small screens, and the whole row stacks below the attribution under 720px.
