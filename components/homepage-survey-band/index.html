Distinct from .gv-ctaband (centered single-column proposals band) — a new variant, no base mutated.

Theming: the tint and CTA derive from --gv-tenant-primary, so ?theme= (or overriding that one var) re-skins the whole band. The preview artwork is a page-local reconstruction (no gv-* class, no brand hex) standing in for the live /SentimentQuestionPreview.png.
